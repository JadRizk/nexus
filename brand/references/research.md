# Research

What the system entered, and what it took or refused. Checked 2026-10-09.

| Reference                                            | What it is                                                                                                                                 | Taken                                                                                | Refused                                                                                                             |
| ---------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| [Arwes](https://arwes.dev/docs)                      | A sci-fi UI framework for React: frames, text effects, animation, sound. Self-described alpha; no React strict mode, no Server Components. | The vector frame as a structural device.                                             | The sound layer, animation as the default state, and the absent contrast floor. Primitives without a component set. |
| [augmented-ui](https://augmented-ui.com/docs/)       | A CSS layer of clipped, chamfered corners and borders through custom properties.                                                           | The idea that the frame belongs in CSS, not in images.                               | The clipped corner. Nexus corners are square, and the frame is drawn as ticks _on_ the corner, not cut out of it.   |
| [Cyberdeck](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cyberdeck) | The operator's console in William Gibson's _Neuromancer_ (1984), now a generic word for hand-built portable computers.                     | The name and the register: an instrument someone operates, not a page someone reads. | Cyberpunk set-dressing: rain, kanji, neon pink as decoration. Pink is restricted to alarm.                          |
| The prototype (`hud` theme)                          | The proof of concept the system grew from. Muted text at 2.14:1, a 9 Hz blink.                                                             | Every signature colour, unchanged.                                                   | Its contrast and its flash rate. It ships as an opt-in theme that is required to fail.                              |
| WCAG 2.2: 1.4.1, 1.4.3, 1.4.11, 2.3.1                | Use of colour, text contrast, non-text contrast, three flashes.                                                                            | All four as build-time or test-time gates.                                           | Nothing.                                                                                                            |
