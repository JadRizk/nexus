Graph 2.0 Build Brief

One graph, built from two. qrntn's renderer, camera and layout are the base, and Nexus's hardening is re-applied on top. The part neither graph has is the reason for the rewrite: a way to travel the graph by its connections, by keyboard and by screen reader, with a way back.

Try the navigation model

A working prototype of the key map in the spec, on a small graph of this design system. Tab into the graph, browse a node's connections with ← →, follow one with Enter, and go back with Backspace. The right-hand panel shows what a screen reader would hear. The buttons under the graph run the same actions, the way the visible key hints will for mouse and touch users.
